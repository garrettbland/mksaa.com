### Big Annual Event Sale Details

Get ready for the biggest celebration of the year! Mid Kansas Auto Auction’s Big Annual Event returns on Tuesday, July 14th & Wednesday, July 15th, and we’re gearing up for two days packed with food, music, big runs, and unbeatable summertime energy.

**Tuesday Night - July 14th**
We’re kicking things off with a car-show–style evening in the lanes, complete with great dinner, live music, and a relaxed summer vibe perfect for celebrating our amazing dealers. Think chrome, classics, community, and cold drinks - the ideal way to start the festivities before sale day.

**Wednesday - July 15th**
Doors open at 7:00 AM with free coffee and breakfast for registered dealers. The sale begins at 9:00 AM sharp, featuring one of the largest run lists of the year, fresh inventory, and serious action in the lanes.

Event t-shirts will be handed out at check-in Wednesday morning (first come, first served), and food trucks will be available on-site for lunch so you can stay close to the action all day long.

🎁 Plus - we’ll be giving away exciting prizes! Details are still under wraps, but we promise you won’t want to miss your chance to win. Stay tuned for more info!

Remember, MKSAA hosts sales every Wednesday in Wichita, but our monthly special events - like the Big Annual Event Sale - takes everything to the next level with more inventory, more fun, and more reasons to be here. If you’re not yet registered, call 316-500-7700 and we’ll get you in the system. We’d love to earn your business!

*These events are not open to the public -- registered auto dealers only.

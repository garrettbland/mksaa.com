### The Time is Right Anniversary Sale Event Details

Get ready to come on down to Mid Kansas Auto Auction for our biggest celebration of the year - The Time Is Right Anniversary Sale! We’re rolling out a brand-new theme for next year’s annual event, complete with two days of fun, food, and high-energy auction action.

**Tuesday Night Kickoff - Dinner & Celebration**
The excitement begins on Tuesday evening, November 11th, with a catered dinner and drinks at 6:00 PM to kick off the celebration. Join fellow dealers for networking, good food, and great vibes to set the stage for an unforgettable two-day event. For dealers flying in, we’ll have complimentary shuttles running from the airport to the event and back to your hotel - making travel smooth from touchdown to check-in.

**Wednesday - The Time is Right Anniversary Sale**
Then, on Wednesday, November 12th, we officially open the doors at 7:00 AM with free coffee and pastries for registered dealers, giving you plenty of time to walk the lot and preview the day’s lineup. The sale goes live at 9:00 AM sharp, and you can expect a high-volume run list that brings serious buying and selling opportunities. Stick around after the sale for a complimentary hot lunch, and stay tuned - major giveaways and the return of fan-favorite surprises are coming, all themed around The Time Is Right. Details will be revealed as the event draws closer!

**Don’t Miss It**
Remember, we have sales every Wednesday in Wichita, but our Anniversary Sale takes things to the next level - bigger crowds, bigger energy, big-ticket giveaways, and a can’t-miss experience. If you’re not yet registered, be sure to reach out ahead of time so we can get you set up and ready to join the fun. We’d love to earn your business: 316-500-7700.

*These events are not open to the public -- registered auto dealers only.

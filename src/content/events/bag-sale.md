### Bag Sale Event Details

Bag Sale - Test Your Luck and Win Big!

It’s almost time for one of the most talked-about promo events of the year - the Bag Sale returns to Mid Kansas Auto Auction on Wednesday, August 12th! This high-energy sale brings suspense, strategy, and serious reward potential, and you’ll want to be in the lanes when the excitement unfolds. The sale kicks off at 9:00 AM sharp, and doors open early at 7:00 AM with free hot coffee and breakfast for registered dealers. Arrive early to walk the lot, preview inventory, and get settled before the action begins.

At the end of the sale, ten lucky dealers will each receive a premium, airline-approved carry-on bag (valued at $125), each containing a mystery cash prize ranging from $1 to $2,000. In true “Deal or No Deal” fashion, winners can choose to keep their bag or auction it off to the highest bidder.

Mystery Cash Prizes:
$1 • $5 • $10 • $20 • $50 • $100 • $250 • $500 • $1,000 • $2,000

We host sales every Wednesday, but special promo events like the Bag Sale raise the stakes with giveaways, entertainment, and great inventory.

If you’re not registered with us yet, call 316-500-7700, and we’ll get you in the system. We’d love to earn your business!

*These events are not open to the public -- registered auto dealers only.

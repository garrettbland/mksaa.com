### Corn Roast Sale Event Details

Nothing brings people together like a fresh corn roast, and we’re serving up all the flavor at the Corn Roast Sale on Wednesday, September 16th at Mid Kansas Auto Auction. The sale kicks off at 9:00 AM sharp, but be sure to arrive early - doors open at 7:00 AM, and registered dealers can fuel up with free hot coffee and breakfast before walking the lot and previewing the run list.

Stick around after the sale for FREE roasted corn and slow-smoked pulled pork sandwiches, hot off the grill. It’s our way of saying thank you for another great day in the lanes and the perfect chance to unwind, connect, and enjoy great food with great company.

We host sales every Wednesday in Wichita, but special events like the Corn Roast Sale take things up a notch with extra fun, good food, and unbeatable energy. Whether you’re in-lane or online, you won’t want to miss it. If you’re not registered yet, reach out ahead of time - we’d love to earn your business. Call 316-500-7700 to get set up.

*These events are not open to the public -- registered auto dealers only.

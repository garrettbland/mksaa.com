### Helping Hands Benefit Sale Event Details

Join us for the **Helping Hands Benefit Sale** on **Wednesday, June 17, 2026**!

This special event is all about giving back, and we invite you to participate in making a meaningful difference. Dealers can sign up to contribute a portion of their sale fees, and **for every vehicle sold during the sale, MKSAA will donate to Wichita's Littlest Heroes.**

Wichita's Littlest Heroes offers free events, financial assistance, art therapy, online support, and more to children ages 0-18. Their mission is clear: **No One Fights Alone**. They are passionate about changing the landscape of pediatric resources in Kansas and creating a brighter future for these children.

The sale starts at 9:00 AM sharp. As always, doors open at 7:00 AM. Registered dealers can join us early for free breakfast and coffee before the action begins. Plus, a food truck will be on-site for lunch!

**At the next sale on June 24th**, we'll present the check to Wichita's Littlest Heroes, and the total raised will be a surprise! We'll reveal the amount at that time, so stay tuned.

Want to be part of this movement? Let us know, and we'll help you get signed up! Your contributions will make a real difference in the lives of Kansas children who need it most. Learn more about Wichita's Littlest Heroes at [www.wichitaslittlestheroes.com](https://www.wichitaslittlestheroes.com).

Remember, MKSAA hosts sales **every Wednesday** in Wichita, but our monthly special events - like the Helping Hands Benefit Sale - offer extra fun, prizes, and the opportunity to make a meaningful impact. If you're not registered with us yet, call **316-500-7700**, and we'll get you in the system. **We'd love to earn your business!**

*These events are not open to the public - registered auto dealers only.*

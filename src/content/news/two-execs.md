Trevor Ottley, Owner and General Manager, and Austin Coyan, Chief Financial Officer, at Mid Kansas Auto Auction, have demonstrated their commitment to professional growth and excellence by successfully completing Auction Academy. The curriculum, designed for auction personnel on the path to leadership positions, is a multi-year educational certification that equips participants with the skills and knowledge necessary to excel in the auction industry.

Auction Academy is a highly regarded program that provides training, networking opportunities, and exposure to industry best practices. Participants engage in a broad curriculum that covers various aspects of the auction business, including management, compliance, operations, marketing, and finance.

Trevor Ottley, Owner and General Manager of Mid Kansas Auto Auction said, "Auction Academy has been fun, educational, and worthwhile, allowing us to see first-hand what many other successful auctions do. We're excited to take what we've learned and use it to benefit our dealers at Mid Kansas Auto Auction in Wichita, keeping us at the top of our industry."

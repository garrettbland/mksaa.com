As Fall approaches and state fairs are happening all over the country Mid Kansas Auto Auction wanted to bring a little of that goodness to our hard-working dealers. The first annual Corn Roast promotional sale was held on September 15th.  Corn roaster Kansas Korn joined us and roasted 350 ears of corn for everyone to enjoy. They taught everyone how to shuck, dip, and indulge in the treat on the spot in between approximately 450 cars running in five lanes. We look forward to having them back next year as roasted sweet corn and beautiful Kansas weather was enjoyed by those of all ages!

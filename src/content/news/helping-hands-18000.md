OH. MY. GOODNESS. We couldn't be any more PROUD of all our dealers, partners, and employees who stepped up in a BIG way to support Wichita's Littlest Heroes! Some donated items to auction off, others made pledges for every car sold that day, and others just wrote a check. Regardless of HOW you contributed, we are so grateful to everyone who helped make this special moment possible. After raising approximately $13,000 in 2022, it was a BIG deal to present an even larger donation totaling $18,049.37 to the local nonprofit that supports children and families battling childhood cancer. Thank you, thank you, THANK YOU to all who made this possible!
